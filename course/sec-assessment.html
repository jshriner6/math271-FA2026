    Section Assignments, Assessments, and Grades

    Homework.

    Working exercises and asking questions is an important way to engage in our course content. Homework exercises will be assigned bi-weekly. They will not be collected for a grade, but they will be very important in preparing for in-class quizzes.
    🔗
    Making mistakes and correcting them is a vital part of the learning process. The assigned homework exercises are designed to be a low-stakes opportunity for this. Start early, engage with your peers, and ask questions!
    🔗
    🔗

    Quizzes.

    We will have five in-class quizzes roughly bi-weekly, on Fridays. The quiz questions will be motivated by the assigned homework exercises, and you can bring your work with you to reference during the quiz.
    🔗
    In general, makeup quizzes will not be considered, unless you have a University excused absence. We will also consider requests from student case management.
    🔗
    🔗

    Labs.

    While lecture will be a part of content delivery, there will also be many opportunities to actively engage with the content. Our time together during most Fridays will be dedicated to active engagement on content that has recently been covered in class. This will typically involve group tasks.
    🔗
    You will receive a grade for your work on labs based on participation/completion. These are great opportunities to make mistakes and ask questions. Much of the benefit of labs comes from your active engagement with others. Therefore, in general, makeup labs will not be considered. However, we will drop your lowest three lab grades, so you can miss up to three labs for any reason without penalty in the gradebook.
    🔗
    🔗

    In general, makeup midterms will be administered during the final exam (see the replacement policy below), unless you have a University excused absence. We will also consider requests from student case management.
    🔗
    🔗

    Final.

    The final exam is scheduled for Tuesday, December 15 from 7:30am -- 9:30am, in our normal classroom.
    🔗
    The final will be cumulative, split into three sections corresponding to content covered on each of the midterms and content not covered on the midterms. We know that it can take time to learn new concepts. Therefore, if your percentage score in either of the first two sections exceeds your score on the corresponding midterm, we will replace the midterm score with your section score from the final.
    🔗
    🔗
