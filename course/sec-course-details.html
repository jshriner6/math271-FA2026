    Course Content and Structure.

    Our course covers a wide variety of topics in mathematics, all of which are useful in physical chemistry. Our topics can be roughly categorized into four sections.
    1. Functions. Complex-valued functions, orthogonal families of functions, multi-variable functions, series representations of functions, integration of functions
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Multi-variable Calculus. Derivatives, multiple integration (in various coordinate systems)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. Linear Algebra. Vectors, vector spaces, bases, matrices, linear systems, linear transformations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors
      🔗
      🔗
    4. Differential Equations. ODEs, PDEs, separation of variables, boundary conditions, series solutions
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    Given the breadth of topics we will cover, our focus will be building intuition and a workable knowledge of the structures and notation involved. Our goal is that you will have a strong enough foundation in these topics that when you see mathematical notation in a physical chemistry context, you will have some perspective on what that notation means and why it is being used. We will work to accomplish this through exposure to the content in a variety of different formats, described in Assignments, Assessments, and Grades.
